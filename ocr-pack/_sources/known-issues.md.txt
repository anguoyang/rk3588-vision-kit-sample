# Known Issues

## Recognition INT8 is not an official recipe

The RKNN Model Zoo ships the recognition model FP-only (no
normalization). This pack's INT8 recognition build keeps the same
no-normalization configuration and quantizes with the zoo's COCO-subset
calibration. It is measured and works, but it is **our** recipe, not an
official one — treat its accuracy on your documents as unverified until
you run your own samples. This divergence is disclosed here rather than
discovered by you.

## Reshape fallback in recognition

Both recognition builds execute one `Reshape` (after the final softmax)
on CPU: 2.58% (FP) / 2.61% (INT8) of frame time. It does not block
deployment; budget for it.

## Detection INT8 p95 spread

Detection INT8 shows a wider p95 (39.99 ms against a 31.39 ms p50) than
its FP sibling. Under a hard real-time budget, prefer FP detection or
leave headroom.

## Character confusions observed

The synthetic end-to-end test produced two confusions (O→0, I→l) out of
38 characters — typical for this model class on synthetic fonts, and
the reason this pack does not quote a character accuracy figure for
real documents.
