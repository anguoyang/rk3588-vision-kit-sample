# Edge OCR Deployment Pack for RK3588

Offline OCR that runs entirely on the board. This pack contains the
PP-OCRv4 detection + recognition pipeline converted for RK3588 (FP and
INT8), a worked end-to-end example, and the on-device measurements —
for products whose documents must never leave the premises.

## Contents

| Component | Contents |
|---|---|
| `models/` | PP-OCRv4-Det and PP-OCRv4-Rec, FP + INT8 (4 artifacts, SHA-256 in `LICENSES.md`) |
| `example_ocr_ppocrv4/` | End-to-end example: detect → recognize → text out |
| `data/reports/` | Raw measurement reports for all 4 artifacts |
| Documentation (this site, also bundled) | Quickstart, benchmarks, known issues, licensing |

## Measured on the reference board

| Artifact | p50 | p95 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| PP-OCRv4-Det FP | 47.86 ms | 49.99 ms | NPU_ONLY |
| PP-OCRv4-Det INT8 | 31.39 ms | 39.99 ms | NPU_ONLY |
| PP-OCRv4-Rec FP | 22.68 ms | 26.45 ms | MIXED (2.58% CPU) |
| PP-OCRv4-Rec INT8 | 14.16 ms | 16.96 ms | MIXED (2.61% CPU) |

Environment: RK3588, Ubuntu 22.04.5, kernel 6.1.118, driver 0.9.8,
librknnrt 2.3.2, toolkit2 2.3.2. Protocol and caveats in
[Benchmarks](benchmarks.md) and [Known Issues](known-issues.md).

## Scope — read before buying

- The recognition model is the stock PP-OCRv4 dictionary (Chinese,
  English, digits). End-to-end verification in this pack: a synthetic
  38-character test image read back **36/38 characters** (two confusions,
  O→0 and I→l, recorded in the example output).
- **Japanese receipt accuracy has not been measured yet.** A
  Japanese-document evaluation is on the roadmap; this pack makes no
  Japanese accuracy claim, and the price reflects that. If your use case
  is Japanese receipts, evaluate the sample numbers against your own
  documents first, or wait for the evaluation edition.

## Why a pack, not the model zoo download

The zoo gives you the models. This pack gives you the pair already
converted for the pinned toolkit/runtime combination, an example that
runs as shipped, the measured latency/fallback record for capacity
planning, and one documented place for the two traps that bite everyone
(the recognition INT8 recipe divergence and the Reshape fallback).
