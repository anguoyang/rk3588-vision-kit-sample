Edge OCR Deployment Pack for RK3588

Offline OCR that runs entirely on the board. This pack contains the PP-OCRv4 detection + recognition pipeline converted for RK3588 (FP and INT8), a worked end-to-end example, and the on-device measurements — for products whose documents must never leave the premises.

Environment: RK3588, Ubuntu 22.04.5, kernel 6.1.118, driver 0.9.8, librknnrt 2.3.2, toolkit2 2.3.2. Protocol and caveats in Benchmarks and Known Issues.

Scope — read before buying

  • The recognition model is the stock PP-OCRv4 dictionary (Chinese, English, digits). End-to-end verification in this pack: a synthetic 38-character test image read back 36/38 characters (two confusions, O→0 and I→l, recorded in the example output).

  • Japanese receipt accuracy has not been measured yet. A Japanese-document evaluation is on the roadmap; this pack makes no Japanese accuracy claim, and the price reflects that. If your use case is Japanese receipts, evaluate the sample numbers against your own documents first, or wait for the evaluation edition.

Why a pack, not the model zoo download

The zoo gives you the models. This pack gives you the pair already converted for the pinned toolkit/runtime combination, an example that runs as shipped, the measured latency/fallback record for capacity planning, and one documented place for the two traps that bite everyone (the recognition INT8 recipe divergence and the Reshape fallback).
