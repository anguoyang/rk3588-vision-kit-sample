Benchmarks

Protocol

Every number below was produced the same way, on the reference configuration:

  • Warmup: 10 runs, then 50 timed runs per session

  • Sessions: 3 independent sessions, each in its own process; the reported value is the median of session medians

  • Priming: one sacrificial load + 5-run cycle in a separate process before measuring (see the first-context effect)

  • Timer: NPU PERF_RUN profiling; CPU-fallback share computed from per-operator timings

  • Verdict: NPU_ONLY = no operator executed on CPU; MIXED = some operators fell back (share shown)

Single-session numbers on this SoC can sit ~15% off the sustained state; that is why the protocol above is not optional when comparing.

How to read this table

  • INT8 is not automatically faster. RetinaFace INT8 is slower than its FP build on this stack and is shipped as a measurement record; the FP artifact is the recommended one. Details in Known Issues.

  • MIXED is not broken. A few Reshape operators routinely fall back to CPU with a small time share. What matters is the share, which is printed for every model — most vendor tables omit it.

  • Full raw reports (per-operator timings, memory deltas, session-level data) are in the Complete package. The summary above is complete and free; the raw files are what you re-check a procurement decision against.
