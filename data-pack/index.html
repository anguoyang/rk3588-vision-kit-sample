RK3588 Edge Measurement Data Pack — Vision Edition

Measured, not estimated. This package contains the complete raw measurement record behind the RK3588 vision leaderboard: 25 configurations across 14 models, every one measured on real RK3588 hardware under a published protocol, with per-operator CPU-fallback data and the failures left in.

This documentation is free. The package itself — the raw report files, the aggregated leaderboard JSON, and the reproduction harness notes — is the paid deliverable.

What this data is for

  • Model selection for an RK3588 product, without buying a board and spending two weeks measuring: latency distributions, memory deltas, and CPU-fallback shares per model and precision.

  • Procurement and review evidence: every figure carries its board fingerprint and protocol block, so a third party can judge what the number is worth and reproduce it.

  • Regression baselines: pin these numbers, re-measure after a toolkit or driver upgrade, and see exactly what moved.

What this data is not

  • Not accuracy benchmarks. Latency and fallback were measured; per-model accuracy claims are explicitly out of scope and marked as such in every report that touches calibration.

  • Not transferable to other SoCs. An RK3588 number says little about RK3576 or any other chip; the environment page states the exact configuration every number belongs to.

Editions

This is the Vision Edition (vision models on RK3588). An LLM/VLM edition, measured under the same protocol discipline, is in preparation.
