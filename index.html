Measured environment

All numbers on this site were measured on one documented configuration:

  • SoC / board: Rockchip RK3588

  • OS: Ubuntu 22.04.5 LTS, kernel 6.1.118

  • Runtime: librknnrt 2.3.2 · Driver: rknpu 0.9.8

  • Conversion: rknn-toolkit2 2.3.2 (x86_64 host)

See the Version Matrix for the exact tested combinations, and Benchmarks for the measurement protocol. Numbers measured outside this matrix are marked as such wherever they appear.

Why not just do it yourself?

You can. The official guides are free. What they don’t give you:

  • Artifacts that are already verified on hardware, with SHA-256 hashes.

  • The failure records — which INT8 calibration silently collapses a detector’s confidence scores, which artifact balloons to 18 MB because of a compiler memory-planning quirk, which ops fall back to CPU and by how much. See Known Issues.

  • A version matrix that is known to work as a set, instead of three days of toolkit/driver/runtime mismatch debugging.

That is what the package sells: the two weeks, already spent.
